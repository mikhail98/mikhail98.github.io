Labsy — Privacy Policy
Effective date: 12 October 2026

Labsy ("the app") is developed by Mikhail Yarashevich ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app processes, what stays on your phone, what is sent to the services we use, and the choices you have. Contact: yrshvch.studio@gmail.com

1. In short
Your blood test results stay on your phone. Labsy has no accounts and no servers that receive your results.
To run subscriptions, count usage, fix crashes and measure our marketing, the app uses a few third-party services. They receive technical and usage data — never your test results or health profile.
2. Data that stays on your device
The following is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone and is never sent to us or to third parties:

your profile: biological sex, birth year, height, weight and unit system;
the markers you track and your test results: values, units, dates, lab names and reference ranges.
You decide when to export this data: "Export to CSV" and "Back up" create files in a location you choose with the system file picker. These files are not encrypted, so store and share them carefully. You can erase everything in Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Data collected through third-party services
App activity: which screens and features you use (for example "results added", "theme changed", "paywall closed") and simple counts (for example how many markers you selected). These events never contain your test values, marker results or profile.
Device and app information: device model, Android version, language, app version, and identifiers such as the Firebase installation ID, app instance ID and, where Android allows it, the advertising ID.
Crash reports and diagnostics: technical details of crashes and errors.
Purchase information: the subscription you bought, its status, price, currency and transaction identifiers. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment card details.
Approximate location: country or city derived from your IP address by analytics providers. We do not collect precise location.
4. Why we use this data
to provide and restore your Premium subscription;
to understand how the app is used and improve it;
to find and fix crashes;
to measure the results of our advertising campaigns (for example, how many installs an ad brought).
We do not sell your data and do not use it to show ads inside Labsy — the app contains no ads.

6. Advertising ID
The advertising ID is used for analytics and to measure advertising campaigns. You can reset or delete it at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).

7. Retention
Analytics and crash data are kept according to each provider's retention settings (for example, Firebase Analytics keeps user-level data for up to 14 months and Crashlytics keeps crash data for 90 days). Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide the subscription and as required by law. Data stored on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

8. Your choices and rights
Delete your results at any time in Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
Ask us to delete data linked to your device identifiers, or to tell you what data we hold: write to yrshvch.studio@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to or restrict its processing.
9. Children
Labsy is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete any related data held by our services.

10. Security
All data sent to the services above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox; files you export or back up are under your control.

11. Health information
Labsy is a personal tracker, not a medical device. Your health data (test results and profile) is processed only on your device. We never receive it, sell it or use it for advertising.

12. Changes
We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the app or on its store page.
